## Approved content principles

- A project's subtitle answers "What is this project?" before it tries to summarize the owner's product insight.
- Personal projects should be introduced as personal projects, with concrete scope and current maturity. Do not force team/product launch language onto an architecture exploration.
- Team projects identify the team context and the owner's actual joining/contribution path.
- Product language belongs in the problem framing, product judgment, user journey, trade-offs and evidence—not as a replacement for a concrete project introduction.
- Visuals must clarify the subject. Distinguish real product screenshots, source evidence, and illustrative/AI-generated diagrams.
- Every detail page must be reachable from its card, have a visible return-to-work/home navigation, and support direct URL/hash entry and browser history.
- Never imply official maintainer, employee, or internal team status without evidence. Use accurate descriptions of sustained collaboration and responsibilities.
